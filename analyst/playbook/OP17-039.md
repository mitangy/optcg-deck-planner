## Game plan
Mono-blue card advantage with almost no counter values on characters. The leader's [When Attacking] trades 1 card for a reveal; since nearly every card in the deck has the Rocks Pirates type (events and Stage included), it draws 2 most of the time. You defend with events, on-attack effects, attack locks and the Captain John forced-target wall, then win late with a hand far bigger than the opponent's.

Leader text: "[When Attacking] You may trash 1 card from your hand: Reveal 1 card from the top of your deck. If the revealed card's type includes "Rocks Pirates", draw 2 cards." Five life, 5000 power.

## Key cards
- OP17-044 Captain John: while rested (rest it with its own draw/trash ability) the opponent can attack only John. The defensive core.
- OP17-045 Kyo: 2-cost draw 1; if one of your characters would be removed by an opponent's effect, trash 2 cards from hand instead. Protects John.
- OP17-054 Miss Buckingham Stussy: on play, a base-cost-6-or-less character cannot attack through their next turn; can repeat by resting 3 DON!! and itself.
- OP17-046 Gloriosa: 4-cost [Blocker] that sends a cost-5-or-less character to the bottom of the owner's deck.
- OP17-049 Charlotte Linlin: opponent chooses you draw 2 or they trash 2; also +1000 on defense by trashing 1.
- OP17-040 Edward.Newgate: draw 1; once per turn trash 1 to give the leader +3000 for a battle (attack or defense).
- OP17-048 Shiki: 7-cost 9000 with [Rush: Character]; −3000 to an enemy character on attack or on the opponent's attack.
- OP17-118 Rocks.D.Xebec: 10-cost, draw 1 and play two differently named Rocks Pirates with total cost 9 or less from hand. +2000 counter in hand if all your characters are counterless.
- OP17-055 There's No Authority in the World That Lasts Forever!!!: [Main] rest 1 DON!! to make your Rocks leader [Unblockable]; [Counter] +2000.
- OP17-056 Rocks Pirates: [Main] rest 5 DON!! to bounce a cost-6-or-less character; [Counter] +2000.
- OP17-050 Streusen and OP08-051 Buckin: cheap Rocks bodies that smooth the curve. Buckin has a 1000 counter, which switches off OP17-118's in-hand counter while Buckin is on the field.
- OP17-042 Kaido: 4-cost [Blocker], −3000 if you reveal 3 Rocks Pirates cards.

## Mulligan
- Keep: any hand with a 1-2 cost (Streusen, Kyo, Buckin) plus one of John, Stussy, Gloriosa or Linlin. Counter events are welcome but not required.
- Ship: hands of only 6+ costs, or hands without a 4-5 cost by turn 3.
- John is the card you most want by turn 3-4 against aggressive decks (Ace, Sabo, Black Luffy).

## Lines and cheese
- Every leader attack is card advantage. Attack with the leader each turn even into bad math; trash your weakest card (excess 10-costs early, extra Buckins late).
- Turn 3-4 (4-6 DON!!): Kyo + Captain John, rest John with its ability at end of turn. The opponent's whole turn is now "beat 6000 John" first. Back John with Linlin's +1000 and the +2000 events. Kyo blocks removal-based answers.
- Stussy on a big rushing threat buys a full turn; on a turn with spare DON!!, re-use its activate (rest 3 DON!! and Stussy) for a second lock.
- Turn 10 DON!!: OP17-118 into Shiki (7) + Kyo (2), or Linlin (5) + John (4). That is three bodies and two draws for one card.
- Lethal setup: with the opponent at 1-2 life and few blockers, rest 1 DON!! for OP17-055 to make the leader [Unblockable], attach the remaining DON!!, and use Newgate's +3000 on the attack. The Rocks leader can push 10000+ through a blocker wall.
- Deck count: the leader plus Streusen, Kyo, Newgate and Linlin draw a lot. In long games count your deck; you can lose to deck-out.

## Matchups
Matchup calls are my judgement, informed by a ranked-play sample on opdecks.xyz (snapshot 2026-09-10) and card reading. Treat them as provisional.

### vs OP09-062 Nico Robin
Rocks favoured. Robin's trigger value matters less when Rocks out-draws her, and John stops her from aiming leader attacks. Watch Robin OP09-107 and Linlin OP17-112 stripping life. Go first.

### vs OP17-079 Monkey.D.Luffy
Luffy favoured in the data and in my read: Loki OP17-119 sweeps your cheap Rocks bodies, Kong Gun OP17-098 can K.O. John and Stussy together (Kyo can save each one for 2 cards from hand), and its many 5000-power 2-costs swarm past one John. Loki and the Elbaph giants gain +12 cost, so Gloriosa and the Rocks Pirates bounce cannot touch them; beat them in battle or lock them with Stussy (base cost still counts for Stussy). Keep Kyo up. Thin sample.

### vs OP13-004 Sabo
Rocks favoured. Sabo needs leader and wide attacks; John plus Stussy blunts both. Its cost-8+ enablers (Docking Six OP15-088, Saul OP17-089, Loki OP17-119) gain cost and dodge Gloriosa and the bounce event, so lock or out-fight them instead; Stussy checks base cost and still works.

### vs OP15-058 Enel
Rocks favoured. Enel caps at 6 DON!! and fights with small attackers that John walls. Beware Trafalgar Law ST10-010 if you hold 7+ cards; trash down before passing.

### vs OP16-001 Portgas.D.Ace
Rocks clearly favoured. Ace's plan is big Rush swings; Stussy and the bounce event handle the 6-cost Marco and Vista, Gloriosa handles LittleOars Jr. and Luffy OP16-015, and John absorbs the turn. Marco OP16-014 can take a removal in another character's place, so remove Marco first. Watch for Newgate OP16-003 double attack turns.

### vs OP14-041 Boa Hancock
Roughly even, slight Rocks edge. Hancock gains life and strips yours; avoid giving her easy K.O.s on Kuja characters. Out-draw her and close with Unblockable leader swings.

### vs OP17-058 Kaido
Rocks slightly favoured. Kaido's attack locks (Queen OP17-065) and −2000 leader pings grind John; your bounce and Gloriosa cannot reach their 9-10 costs (Kaido OP17-063, Linlin ST34-004), so answer those with Shiki's −3000 plus a battle, or simply wall them with John.
