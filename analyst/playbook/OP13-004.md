## Game plan
In OP17, Sabo is mostly a shell for the black Elbaph Straw Hat package plus red support. Saul and Loki (always +12 cost) and Pirates Docking Six (cost 11) satisfy Sabo's "Character with a cost of 8 or more" check. With 1 DON!! on the leader, everything you control gets +1000. Stacked on the Elbaph +3000 from a cost-12+ character, your 2-costs become 6000 attackers. Go wide, pressure every turn, and finish with Rush Luffy.

Leader text: "If you have 4 or more Life cards, give this Leader −1000 power. [DON!! x1] If you have a Character with a cost of 8 or more, your Leader and all of your Characters gain +1000 power." Five life, 5000 base power (4000 while at 4-5 life).

## Key cards
- OP17-089 Jaguar.D.Saul: 4-cost 6000, always +12 cost. Turns on both Sabo's buff and the Elbaph +3000; digs 3 for an Elbaph card.
- OP17-119 Loki: 6-cost 8000 (+3000 on the opponent's turn), cost 18; on play K.O. opposing characters with total cost 4 or less.
- OP15-088 Pirates Docking Six: 5-cost 7000, effective cost 11. Enables Sabo but not the Elbaph 12+ clause. On play mills 3 to play a 2-cost-or-less Straw Hat from trash.
- OP17-080 Usopp / OP17-082 Sanji / OP17-087 Nico Robin / OP17-095 Roronoa Zoro / OP17-083 Jinbe / OP17-086 Nami / OP17-084 Tony Tony.Chopper: the Elbaph Straw Hat 1-2 costs (see the Black Luffy note for what each does).
- ST01-011 Brook: 2-cost 3000 with a 2000 counter; on play gives 2 rested DON!! to the leader, which covers Sabo's DON!! x1 for free.
- OP17-093 Monkey.D.Luffy: 8-cost, [Rush] with a cost-12+ character, draw 1 and replay a 2-cost-or-less from trash.
- OP17-096 I'm Luffy!!...: 1-cost [Counter] +4000 with a cost-12+ character.
- OP04-016 Bad Manners Kick Course: 0-cost [Counter], trash 1 for +3000.
- Missing from card data: P-157 Monkey.D.Luffy (played as a 2-of in the reference list). Do not quote its text.
- Note: Rodo OP17-094 and Gerd OP17-081 only gain +12 cost under an Elbaph leader, so they do nothing for Sabo and are not played.

## Mulligan
- Keep: Saul, or Usopp/Nami to find it, plus 2-3 cheap Straw Hats. Brook is a strong keep (counter plus free DON!!).
- Keep Docking Six hands too; it enables Sabo's buff a turn later than Saul but rebuys a 2-cost.
- Ship: hands that can't put a cost-8+ character down by turn 3-4, and hands heavy in 6-8 costs.

## Lines and cheese
- Early life: at 4-5 life the leader is only 4000. Taking 1-2 early hits instead of countering brings it back to 5000 and keeps counters for later. Do not panic-counter leader attacks on turns 1-3.
- Turn 2 (3 DON!!): Usopp or Sanji plus a 1-cost; Usopp digs for Saul.
- Turn 3 (5 DON!!): Saul (4) + 1 DON!! on the leader. Your 2-costs are now 2000 + 3000 + 1000 = 6000, the leader is 5000-6000 + DON!!. Swing everything.
- Turn 4 (7 DON!!): Loki to clear 4 total cost of small bodies, Brook to feed DON!! to the leader.
- Turn 5+ (8-10 DON!!): Luffy OP17-093 with Rush, replaying Chopper or Brook from trash. Chopper gives [Unblockable] to the biggest attacker.
- Lethal math: with Saul out and the leader powered, a board of four 6000s plus a 7000+ leader forces 2000+ counter per attack. That quickly runs most hands dry.

## Matchups
Matchup calls are my judgement, informed by a ranked-play sample on opdecks.xyz (snapshot 2026-09-10) and card reading. Treat them as provisional.

### vs OP17-039 Rocks.D.Xebec
Rocks favoured. Captain John forces your wide attacks into one 6000 body, and Stussy locks your best attacker. Overload John with several 6000+ swings per turn; Loki can clear Kyo first.

### vs OP09-062 Nico Robin
Even. Race before her Linlin turn turns her 4000 bodies into 8000s. Her life gain (Smoothie, Sweet 3 Generals) stretches the race; keep pressure on the leader, not the board.

### vs OP17-079 Monkey.D.Luffy
Sabo clearly favoured: the same package with +1000 everywhere wins every equal trade. Respect their leader-granted blockers (Rodo, Gerd, Loki); attack with 6000s into 5000s.

### vs OP15-058 Enel
Enel favoured. Its cheap −power events and Lightning Dragon undo your +1000 math, and its 0-cost counters stop your swings. Push early before Ohm/Holly double attacks come online.

### vs OP16-001 Portgas.D.Ace
Slightly Ace. Its 8000 bodies with +2000-counter in hand outsize your 6000s. Win by tempo on turns 2-4; you lose a long game to Newgate double-attack turns.

### vs OP14-041 Boa Hancock
Sabo favoured. Hancock is slower; go wide and don't hand her K.O.s on Kuja 5000+ characters while her leader has DON!!.

### vs OP17-058 Kaido
Slightly Kaido. Queen OP17-065 and Kaido's −2000 leader pings blunt a wide board. Kill early before its 10-costs arrive.
