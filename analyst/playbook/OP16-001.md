## Game plan
Mono-red Whitebeard Pirates "8000-power" deck. Most characters are 8000-power Whitebeard bodies; the leader gives one of them [Rush] each turn, so every big play attacks the turn it lands. Ace OP16-118 turns the 8000s in your hand into +2000 counters, so your hand defends well despite the high curve. The finish is an Edward.Newgate turn that gives your leader [Double Attack].

Leader text: "[Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] Up to 1 of your [Monkey.D.Luffy] Characters or up to 1 of your Characters with a type including "Whitebeard Pirates", with 8000 power or more, gains [Rush] during this turn." Five life, 5000 power.

## Key cards
- OP16-003 Edward.Newgate: 8-cost 10000; on your turn your leader gains [Double Attack] and +2000. On play reveal two 8000 characters to give −6000.
- OP17-005 Edward.Newgate: 10-cost 12000 (−4 cost if the opponent has a 10000+ character); on play your mono-color leader's base power becomes 8000 until the end of the opponent's next turn.
- OP16-004 Curiel / OP17-006 Kingdew: 7-cost vanilla 8000s with a 2000 counter. Rush targets and counters.
- OP16-014 Marco: 6-cost 8000; takes an opponent's removal in place of another character and recurs itself on K.O. by trashing an 8000 from hand.
- OP16-011 Vista: 6-cost 8000; with 1 DON!! on attack K.O. two 2000-base-power-or-less characters.
- OP17-016 Rakuyo: 3-cost; on play K.O. two 2000-base-or-less characters.
- OP16-118 Portgas.D.Ace: 5-cost; your 8000 characters in hand have +2000 counter; on play/on K.O. digs 5 for Luffy or a Whitebeard card.
- OP16-015 Monkey.D.Luffy: costs 2 with an Ace leader and 6+ DON!!; on the opponent's attack, trash an 8000 from hand to make your leader and Luffy 7000 base.
- OP16-017 LittleOars Jr.: 4-cost 8000 [Blocker] (only 4000 without a Whitebeard cost-8+ character).
- OP13-016 Monkey.D.Garp: 1-cost searcher for a 3+ cost card.
- OP16-021 Moby Dick: stage that digs 3; later trash it to give a rested DON!! to the leader.
- OP08-118 Silvers Rayleigh: 8-cost, −3000/−2000 split and a K.O. on a 3000-or-less.
- OP17-017 Ga Ha Ha Ha!!, OP16-020, OP04-016: counter events.

## Mulligan
- Keep: Garp or Moby Dick plus a 3-5 cost (Rakuyo, Ace OP16-118, LittleOars) and one 7-cost.
- Going second is reasonable: you hit 7 and 8 DON!! first.
- Ship: hands that are all 7-10 costs with no early play, and hands without any 8000 to support Ace's counter boost.

## Lines and cheese
- Turn 3-4 (5-7 DON!!): Curiel or Kingdew and Rush it with the leader. An 8000 Rush attacker every turn wins races against 5000-6000 boards.
- Rakuyo and Vista punish base-2000 boards: the Elbaph Straw Hats of Sabo and Black Luffy are base 2000, so each of these K.O.s two of them.
- Newgate OTK (10 DON!!): OP16-003 (8) + Rush it with the leader, 2 DON!! on the leader. Leader becomes 9000 with [Double Attack] and Newgate swings 10000. Lead with Newgate to draw counters, then the leader for 2 Life.
- If the opponent has a 10000+ character (Kaido, Linlin, Rocks OP17-118), OP17-005 costs 6. Its leader buff starts immediately and lasts through their next turn: play it, Rush it as a 12000, and attack with an 8000-base leader plus the remaining DON!!. If an OP16-003 Newgate survived from last turn, the leader is 10000 base-plus-bonus with [Double Attack] before any DON!!, which is the strongest lethal turn in the deck.
- Defense: Luffy OP16-015 (no printed counter) is a 2-cost body whose [On Your Opponent's Attack] trashes an 8000 from hand to make your leader 7000 base. LittleOars only blocks well once a Whitebeard cost-8+ character is out.
- Marco protects your Rush threat from bounce and K.O. effects; play it before the Newgate turn.

## Matchups
Matchup calls are my judgement, informed by a ranked-play sample on opdecks.xyz (snapshot 2026-09-10) and card reading. Treat them as provisional.

### vs OP17-039 Rocks.D.Xebec
Rocks clearly favoured. Captain John soaks your single Rush attacker, Stussy locks it, and Rocks out-draws you. Swing with several threats per turn and keep Marco up against bounce.

### vs OP09-062 Nico Robin
Robin favoured. Her life gain outlasts your double-attack turn and 8000 Big Mom bodies on her turn trade evenly. Hit hard before her Linlin.

### vs OP17-079 Monkey.D.Luffy
Even. Rakuyo and Vista wipe their base-2000 Straw Hats in pairs. Loki gets +3000 only on their turn, so attack it on your turn while it is 8000.

### vs OP13-004 Sabo
Slightly Ace. Same reasoning as Luffy: Rakuyo/Vista are the stars. Their 6000s stall your 8000s only with counters.

### vs OP15-058 Enel
Enel favoured. Its cheap −power events and Lightning Dragon handle one Rush threat per turn. Rakuyo still kills Ohm/Shura/Satori (base 2000) two at a time; lead with it.

### vs OP14-041 Boa Hancock
Hancock favoured. Her Trigger characters and life gain absorb your swings. Avoid K.O.ing her Kuja 5000+ characters while her leader has DON!! attached.

### vs OP17-058 Kaido
Kaido favoured. Its 10-costs enable your cheap OP17-005, but Queen OP17-065's attack lock and Kaido OP17-063's K.O. blunt your Rush threats.
