## Format snapshot (OP17, early October 2026)
- Results aggregated by opdecks.xyz for 23 Aug to 20 Sep 2026 (380 top-finishing lists, mostly shop and store events, JP and EN) put OP14-020 Dracule Mihawk far ahead on share (about 20%), followed by OP09-062 Nico Robin, OP17-039 Rocks.D.Xebec, OP17-079 Monkey.D.Luffy, OP13-004 Sabo, OP15-058 Enel, OP16-001 Portgas.D.Ace, OP14-041 Boa Hancock and OP17-058 Kaido.
- OP14-020 Dracule Mihawk is banned from 2026-10-12 (announced 2026-09-24). Until then expect it everywhere; after that, the decks it held down (Rocks above all, since Rocks has the Slash attribute that turns on Mihawk's +1000) should rise, and decks that farmed Mihawk (Ace, Hancock, Sabo) lose a good matchup.
- Limitless had OP17 regionals listed (Hampton, Ottawa, Utrecht, Athens, Prague, Brisbane, Mexico City, Santiago, plus Dallas and Utrecht Championship Finals) but no standings posted when this was written. Treat play-rate claims as store-level evidence, not big-event evidence.

## DON!! tempo
- The player going first gets 1 DON!! and no draw on turn 1; the player going second gets 2 DON!! and draws. After that each side adds 2 per turn up to 10 (Enel decks cap at 6 because the leader's rule shrinks the DON!! deck).
- Every DON!! you do not spend or attach is wasted tempo, unless you are holding it on purpose for a counter event or a [Blocker]-punishing trick. Plan the turn backwards from what you want on board at end of turn.
- "DON!! −N" costs return DON!! to your DON!! deck, so they shrink next turn's plays unless something refunds them (Kaido OP17-062, Enel's leader refill, Kaido's ramp pieces). Count your DON!! after the effect, not before.
- Ramp is only good if your hand can use it. A ramp turn that leaves you with 1-2 cards in hand often loses to a single board wipe or tax.

## Life as a resource
- Life cards are cards in hand you have not drawn yet. Taking a hit early (especially from a leader attack you could only stop by burning a 2000 counter) is usually correct, and it feeds Triggers.
- Stop taking free hits around 2 life. From there on, every hit can be the last and Trigger luck decides games.
- Leaders with life-based thresholds change the math: Sabo OP13-004 is 1000 weaker at 4+ life, Hancock's Rayleigh OP14-108 and Shakuyaku OP14-107 want the opponent at 3 or fewer life, Robin's character OP09-107 wants the opponent at 3 or more.
- Life you add (Borsalino EB04-058, Marshall.D.Teach OP16-119, Sweet 3 Generals OP17-114) is real defense, but face-down life added from the deck is random; do not count on a Trigger from it.

## Counter math
- An attack succeeds if the attacker's power is equal to or higher than the target's. Defender must end strictly above.
- Size attacks so the defender has to overspend: if their leader is 5000 and you attack with 7000, they need 3000 of counter, which usually means a 2000 plus a 1000 (two cards) instead of one. Attacking at exactly 6000 lets a single 2000 counter stop it.
- Count their hand, not just your attacks. Four cards in hand with average 1000-1500 counter is roughly 5000-6000 of defense, plus events.
- Swing the biggest threat first when they must choose; swing the smallest first when you want to bait counters before the real attacker.
- Counterless decks (Rocks, much of Kaido, Black Luffy's 6-8 cost giants) defend with events and on-attack effects instead. Read the deck, not the card count.

## Going first vs second
- First: you set the board but your first attack is a turn later. Good for decks with strong 1-3 cost setup (Rocks, Black Luffy, Enel).
- Second: an extra card and two DON!! on turn 1, and you hit the big DON!! counts first. Good for decks with a 10-cost finisher or a turn-2 power spike.
- Most current lists still choose first by default. Pick second when your deck wants the 10 DON!! turn first or the opponent's turn-2 play punishes a thin board.

## Blocker walls and lethal through them
- Blockers buy time only if they survive. Removal, rest effects (Mamaragan OP15-078, Kaido EB04-030, Shanks OP17-022 rests their whole board), "cannot activate [Blocker]" (Perfume Femur OP07-057) and [Unblockable] (Chopper OP17-084, Rocks event OP17-055) all walk past them.
- Black Luffy turns its cost-12-or-more characters into blockers, and Loki OP17-119 is 11000 on the opponent's turn: count those before planning lethal.
- Rocks' Captain John OP17-044 is a forced-target wall: while it is rested, the opponent can attack only it. Remove or beat it first; nothing else gets through that turn.

## When to take damage vs counter
- Take it: early leader attacks, attacks that need two counter cards, any attack when you are at 4-5 life and holding your hand for a key turn.
- Counter it: attacks on a character you need next turn, the attack that would put you to 1-0 life, and attacks you can stop with an event that also gives value (draw or debuff).
- Do not counter a character attack to save a blocker you will lose anyway on their next removal.

## Reading the opponent
- DON!! left active at the end of their turn usually means a counter event or [On Your Opponent's Attack] effect (Kaido leader DON!! −1, Shiki OP17-048, Linlin OP17-049). 0-cost counter events (Enel's El Thor and friends, Bad Manners Kick Course, the Rocks events) need no DON!!, so open DON!! is not the only tell.
- Big hands vs Enel's Trafalgar Law ST10-010 (trashes 2 if you hold 7+) and Kaido's X.Drake OP17-075: keep hand size in mind against purple.
- A player who refuses to attack with their leader is often sandbagging counters for a lethal turn.

## Common cheese patterns
- OTK turns: double attackers (Ace with Newgate OP16-003, Enel's Ohm OP15-061 with Holly OP15-071), mass Rush (Ace leader, Luffy OP17-093 once a cost-12+ character is out), and pump-plus-unblockable leaders (Rocks event OP17-055).
- Lethal through blockers: rest or remove blockers first, then attack with Unblockable/"no Blocker" effects last when the opponent has no hand left.
- Life manipulation: effects that move the opponent's top Life card to their hand (Hancock leader OP14-041, Hancock OP14-112, Linlin OP17-112) and Robin's Banish leader take life without letting Triggers fire. Nico Robin OP09-107 trashes the top Life card outright.
- Deck-out: you lose when your deck runs out. Rocks draws 2-3 extra per turn and Black Luffy mills itself with Usopp OP17-080 and Saul OP17-089; long games can end on deck count. Count decks past turn 8.
- Attack locks: Miss Buckingham Stussy OP17-054, Queen OP17-065 and Hancock's You'll Frighten Me OP14-118 stop specific characters from attacking; plan threats so one lock does not blank your turn.
