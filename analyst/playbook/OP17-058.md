## Game plan
Purple Animal Kingdom Pirates ramp into 9-10 cost bombs. The leader pays DON!! −1 to give −2000 on attack or on defense, so it shrinks one opposing character every turn cycle. Cheap Animal Kingdom pieces ramp you (Hawkins, Yamato, Queen EB04-032, King EB04-031), and DON!!-returning costs are refunded by Kaido OP17-062. The top end (Kaido OP17-063, Queen OP17-065, Linlin ST34-004, Lead Performers, Kaido EB04-030) removes, locks and outsizes everything. Survive to 10 DON!! with a full hand, then take over.

Leader text: "[When Attacking]/[On Your Opponent's Attack] [Once Per Turn] DON!! −1: Give up to 1 of your opponent's Characters −2000 power during this turn." Five life, 5000 power.

- OP17-073 Basil Hawkins: 3-cost; trash 1 to add an active DON!!. 2000 counter.
- OP17-074 Yamato: 3-cost [Blocker], adds a rested DON!!.

## Mulligan
- Keep: Queen EB04-032 or the search event plus one 3-cost ramp (Hawkins or Yamato). Ramp is the whole early game.
- Keep one 10-cost at most; two is fine only with ramp.
- Ship: hands with no 1-3 cost plays, or no ramp and no search.

## Lines and cheese
- Turn 1-2: Queen EB04-032 (draw 2), Hawkins or Yamato. Every extra DON!! here moves your 10-cost a turn earlier.
- Use the leader's −2000 on defense against your opponent's biggest early attacker; it costs a DON!!, so do it when the DON!! would otherwise sit unused.
- Black Maria turn: +5 rested DON!! cannot pay costs, but they can be returned for DON!! −N effects. Black Maria then Linlin ST34-004 (DON!! −4) or Lead Performers/Queen OP17-065 costs is the key trick. At end of turn you fall back to the opponent's DON!! count.
- Kaido OP17-062 on board turns each "DON!! −N" turn into a near-free effect: the first returned DON!! comes back active and another readies.
- Removal turn (10 DON!!): Kaido OP17-063 (10), then DON!! −1 to negate and K.O. a cost-6-or-less. Or Conquest of the Sea for two K.O.s.
- Lock turn: Queen OP17-065 stops two cost-5-or-less attackers through their turn; pairs with the leader's defensive −2000.

## Matchups
Low confidence overall: small tournament sample (about 19-30 lists) and a ladder sample of about 4,300 games. Calls are my judgement.

### vs OP17-039 Rocks.D.Xebec
Slightly Rocks. Captain John walls your bombs, and Rocks' bounce can't reach your 9-10 costs, but Stussy locks them. In a long game Rocks out-draws you; X.Drake's discard and Queen's locks are how you keep pace.

### vs OP09-062 Nico Robin
Even. Linlin ST34-004 answers her Linlin; Queen locks her small Big Mom attackers. Watch her Life gain.

### vs OP17-079 Monkey.D.Luffy
Luffy favoured. They hit before your ramp lands; Queen OP17-065's lock can't touch their +12-cost giants. Use the leader's −2000 every turn on a 5000 attacker.

### vs OP13-004 Sabo
Kaido favoured. Queen's lock hits their 2-cost attackers, and your leader's −2000 undoes their +1000. Survive to 10 DON!!.

### vs OP15-058 Enel
Even. Your leader's −2000 drops their 2000-power attackers to 0 in battle; their Lightning Dragon can stop your 10-cost from untapping.

### vs OP16-001 Portgas.D.Ace
Kaido favoured. Your 10000+ bodies make their OP17-005 cheaper, but Queen locks and OP17-063 K.O.s their 6-cost Marco/Vista, and Linlin zeroes Newgate.

### vs OP14-041 Boa Hancock
Hancock favoured. Your K.O.s on her Kuja feed her leader; prefer locks and rests over K.O.s.
