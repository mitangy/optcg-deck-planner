## Game plan
Blue/yellow Kuja Trigger control. Your Kuja characters have "[Trigger] play this card", so the opponent's hits on your Life put bodies on your board, and the leader draws a card each time you play a Character on their turn. With 1 DON!! attached to your leader, the first time each turn a Kuja/Amazon Lily character with 5000+ base power is K.O.'d, the opponent's top Life goes to their hand. Removing your characters costs them life. Life setup (Hiyori, Zoro, Borsalino, Hancock OP14-112) and Rayleigh's removal at 3 or fewer opposing Life close the game.

Leader text: "[Opponent's Turn] When you play a Character, draw 1 card. [DON!! x1] [Once Per Turn] When one of your {Amazon Lily} or {Kuja Pirates} type Characters with 5000 base power or more is K.O.'d, add up to 1 card from the top of your opponent's Life cards to the owner's hand." Four life, 5000 power.

- OP14-105 Gorgon Sisters: 6-cost 5000; reveal 3 Kuja/Amazon Lily cards to give the leader and every character a rested DON!!. Trigger: plays itself.

- OP15-113 Roronoa Zoro: 4-cost 6000, trash 1 to add a Life card.
- EB04-058 Borsalino: [Blocker], +1 Life at 2 or fewer.
- OP15-119 Monkey.D.Luffy: 5-cost 7000, Rush at 6+ DON!!, grows when the opponent uses an Event or [Blocker].

## Mulligan
- Keep: Hiyori or Zoro, Ran, and one 5-6 cost Kuja. You want a Kuja body plus a way to get DON!! onto the leader by turn 3.
- Ship: hands without 4-6 cost plays; you are slow and must not fall a turn behind.
- Going second is fine: the extra card and earlier 6-cost Gorgon Sisters/Shakuyaku matter more than tempo.

## Lines and cheese
- Every turn, end with at least 1 DON!! on the leader (attach and attack, or use Ran or Gorgon Sisters). Without it, opponents can kill your Kuja for free.
- Hiyori line: put Ran, Marigold, Gorgon Sisters or Shakuyaku on top of your Life. Their next leader hit plays it for free and the leader draws.
- Punish K.O.s: each turn the first K.O. of a 5000+ Kuja/Amazon Lily body strips a Life card from them, with no Trigger check. Bait removal into Ran (4-cost) rather than your big pieces.
- Rayleigh window: once they are at 3 Life, Rayleigh (or its Trigger) K.O.s a 7000-base body and Shakuyaku draws.
- Closing turn (9-10 DON!!): Hancock OP14-112 strips a Life card and adds one of yours. Perfume Femur on the leader stops their blockers for the last swing.
- You'll Frighten Me at 2 or fewer Life switches off their biggest attacker for the turn.

## Matchups
Matchup calls are my judgement, informed by a ranked-play sample on opdecks.xyz (snapshot 2026-09-10) and card reading. Treat them as provisional.

### vs OP17-039 Rocks.D.Xebec
Slightly Rocks. Rocks' bounce (Gloriosa to the deck bottom, the Rocks Pirates event to hand) removes your Kuja without K.O.ing them, so your leader's life strip never fires. Pressure early and make them use John.

### vs OP09-062 Nico Robin
Robin clearly favoured. Banish means her leader hits never flip your Triggers, and her Linlin strip also skips them. Race with Luffy OP15-119 and hold blockers.

### vs OP17-079 Monkey.D.Luffy
Slightly Hancock. Their Robin −3000 and Loki K.O.s feed your leader; keep DON!! attached. Kong Gun's double K.O. only strips 1 Life (once per turn).

### vs OP13-004 Sabo
Slightly Sabo. A wide 6000 board overwhelms Marigold/Borsalino. Get blockers and Life up before turn 4.

### vs OP15-058 Enel
Hancock clearly favoured. Enel's small removal feeds your leader and its attacks feed your Triggers.

### vs OP16-001 Portgas.D.Ace
Hancock favoured. Single Rush threats per turn fight into Trigger bodies and life gain. Save You'll Frighten Me for the Newgate double-attack turn (target Newgate or another active attacker; the leader is not a character).

### vs OP17-058 Kaido
Hancock favoured. Kaido's −2000 and rest effects don't K.O.; its Kaido OP17-063 does, and feeds your strip. Go long.
