## Game plan
Six-DON!! tempo deck. The leader shrinks your DON!! deck to 6, but from your second turn it refills the field once per turn and dumps 4 rested DON!! onto one character. Almost every card cares about "6 or less DON!! on your field", which is always true for you. You play cheap Sky Island characters and a pile of 0-cost "DON!! −1" events that draw, shrink or K.O. small characters and double as +2000 counters for Enel. You need to win the board before the opponent's 8-10 DON!! turns outclass you.

Leader text: "Under the rules of this game, your DON!! deck consists of 6 cards. [Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] If it is your second turn or later, add up to 1 DON!! card from your DON!! deck and set it as active, and add up to 4 additional DON!! cards and rest them. Then, give up to 4 rested DON!! cards to 1 of your Characters." Five life, 5000 power.

## Mulligan
- Keep: two or more 1-costs (Ohm, Shura, Satori, Pudding) plus Holly or a removal event.
- Keep extra events freely; they are card draw, removal and counters at once.
- Ship: hands with no 1-cost characters (nothing to load the leader's 4 DON!! onto on turn 2).

## Lines and cheese
- Order matters: fire your "DON!! −N" events first, then activate the leader. The leader refills up to 5 DON!! from your DON!! deck, so every DON!! you returned is replaced the same turn.
- Turn 2 (first player: 3 DON!! → leader fills you to 6): Shura plus another 1-cost, leader dumps 4 DON!! on Shura. Shura attacks immediately at 6000 with Rush.
- Turn 3: Holly + Ohm. Next turn Ohm attacks with Double Attack and 4 DON!! from the leader: 6000 for 2 Life.
- Double-attack OTK: with Holly and two Ohms on board, the leader loads one Ohm with 4 DON!!; with 1-2 DON!! spare on the other, two double attacks threaten 4 Life plus the leader swing. Clear blockers first with Gamma Knife or Mamaragan.
- Defense: Holly and Ohm become 6000 base on the opponent's turn, and your 0-cost events counter for free. Keep 2-3 events in hand when you pass.
- Lightning Dragon on a rested 10-cost after it attacks buys a full turn.

## Matchups
Matchup calls are my judgement, informed by a ranked-play sample on opdecks.xyz (snapshot 2026-09-10) and card reading. Treat them as provisional.

### vs OP17-039 Rocks.D.Xebec
Rocks favoured. Captain John stops your small attackers, and Rocks' hand size beats your events in a long game. Law punishes their 7+ card hands; get Gamma Knife on John and swing that turn.

### vs OP09-062 Nico Robin
Slightly Robin. Her Big Mom bodies come back from Life. Pressure early and save Lightning Dragon for Linlin.

### vs OP17-079 Monkey.D.Luffy
Slightly Luffy. Their 5000 2-costs dodge El Thor; their giants are cost-12+ blockers. Kiten/Ohm −1000 plus El Thor can still pick off 4000s. Race with double attacks.

### vs OP13-004 Sabo
Enel favoured. Your cheap −power events break Sabo's +1000 math, and Lightning Dragon/Mamaragan stop wide swings. Kill Saul (6000, in range of Gum-Gum Lightning) to switch off the Elbaph +3000.

### vs OP16-001 Portgas.D.Ace
Enel favoured. Ace needs big DON!! turns; you win by tempo before Newgate. Gamma Knife and Divine Departure handle the 8000s.

### vs OP14-041 Boa Hancock
Hancock clearly favoured. Her Trigger characters replace themselves, she draws on your turn, and your small removal feeds her leader. Avoid K.O.ing Kuja 5000+ bodies while her leader has DON!!.

### vs OP17-058 Kaido
Even. Kaido's leader −2000 on attack and defence makes your 2000-power attackers useless in battle. Its X.Drake and Linlin ST34-004 are slow; win before turn 6.
