## Game plan
Black Elbaph aggro-midrange. Giants that gain +12 cost (Rodo, Gerd, Saul, Loki) switch on the "if there is a Character with a cost of 12 or more" clause on the Straw Hat 1-2 costs, which turns them into 5000-power attackers with extra effects. The leader then gives every cost-12+ character [Blocker], so your giants double as a wall. Win by going wide early, removing blockers with Robin, Loki and Kong Gun, and finishing with Rush Luffy plus Unblockable.

Leader text: "All of your Characters with a cost of 12 or more gain [Blocker]." Five life, 5000 power.

## Key cards
- OP17-094 Rodo: 1-cost, +12 cost with an Elbaph leader. The turn-1 enabler and, via the leader, a 1-cost blocker.
- OP17-081 Gerd: 2-cost 3000, +12 cost; on play trash 1 to return a Character cost 8 or less from trash to hand (Loki, Luffy).
- OP17-089 Jaguar.D.Saul: 4-cost 6000, always +12 cost; on play digs 3 for an Elbaph card (trashes the rest).
- OP17-119 Loki: 6-cost 8000, +12 cost, +3000 on the opponent's turn (11000 blocker); on play K.O. opposing characters with total cost 4 or less.
- OP17-080 Usopp / OP17-082 Sanji / OP17-087 Nico Robin / OP17-095 Roronoa Zoro / OP17-091 Brook / OP17-083 Jinbe: 2-costs that become 5000 with a cost-12+ character. Usopp digs, Sanji filters, Robin gives −3000, Brook makes the opponent discard, Jinbe becomes a blocker, Zoro protects against removal by putting 3 cards from trash on the bottom of your deck.
- OP17-084 Tony Tony.Chopper: 1-cost, gives a character [Unblockable] for the turn (needs a cost-12+ character). The lethal enabler.
- OP17-086 Nami: 1-cost, trash an Elbaph card to draw 2.
- OP17-093 Monkey.D.Luffy: 8-cost 8000, [Rush] with a cost-12+ character; on play draws 1 and replays a 2-cost or less from trash.
- OP15-088 Pirates Docking Six: 5-cost 7000, cost 11; mills 3 to play a 2-cost-or-less Straw Hat from trash.
- OP17-098 Gum-Gum Kong Gun: rest 6 DON!! to K.O. two characters cost 6 or less; [Counter] leader +3000.
- OP17-097 I'll Feed on This Rage...: all opposing characters −1 cost this turn; [Counter] leader +3000.
- OP17-096 I'm Luffy!!...: [Counter] +4000 with a cost-12+ character; Trigger returns an Elbaph card from trash.

## Mulligan
- Keep: Rodo or Gerd plus two 1-2 cost Straw Hats. That is the whole early game.
- Also keep: Saul plus cheap Straw Hats (Saul is your enabler on turn 3).
- Ship: hands with no enabler (no Rodo, Gerd, Saul) and no Usopp/Nami to find one. Without a cost-12+ character your 2-costs are 2000-power vanillas.
- Card-number caveat: some lists also play P-157 Monkey.D.Luffy, which is not in our card data; its text is unverified.

## Lines and cheese
- Turn 1 (1 DON!!): Rodo. Every later 2-cost is now a 5000 attacker, and Rodo is a blocker.
- Turn 2 (3 DON!!): two 1-2 costs, or Gerd plus Nami. Attack with the leader and any Rush you have; most of your board can't attack yet.
- Turn 3-4: Saul to dig, then Loki at 6 DON!! to clear 1-2 small bodies. Play OP17-097 first (1 DON!!) to drop every opposing cost by 1 and let Loki take one more.
- Turn 5+ (7-8 DON!!): OP17-097 then Kong Gun (6 rested DON!!) reaches cost-7 characters for two K.O.s. A strong reset turn against Kaido, Ace and Robin boards.
- Lethal turn (9-10 DON!!): Luffy OP17-093 (Rush, draws, replays Chopper from trash), Chopper makes Luffy or the leader [Unblockable]. Swing small 5000s first to draw counters, Unblockable last.
- Self-mill matters: Usopp and Saul trash cards from your deck, Docking Six mills 3. Zoro's replacement puts cards back, which also slows deck-out.

## Matchups
Matchup calls are my judgement, informed by a ranked-play sample on opdecks.xyz (snapshot 2026-09-10) and card reading. Treat them as provisional.

### vs OP17-039 Rocks.D.Xebec
Luffy favoured. Loki and Kong Gun clear Captain John and the cheap Rocks bodies, and Rocks' bounce and Gloriosa cannot touch your +12-cost giants. Kyo makes removal cost them 2 cards each, which still helps you. Go first.

### vs OP09-062 Nico Robin
Slightly Luffy. Hit before her 10-cost Linlin turn; once 4000 Big Mom bodies become 8000 on her turn your 5000s trade badly. Banish means your Triggers (OP17-096) don't fire off her leader hits.

### vs OP13-004 Sabo
Sabo clearly favoured: it runs the same Elbaph package with +1000 for its whole board and red counters. Its giants sit at cost 11-18 and are out of Kong Gun's reach; aim removal at its 2-cost Straw Hats and try to win the race on board width.

### vs OP15-058 Enel
Slightly Luffy. Enel's −power events and Lightning Dragon trade well with 5000s, but Loki and Rodo blockers soak Ohm/Holly double attacks. Keep a blocker up for the Holly turn.

### vs OP16-001 Portgas.D.Ace
Even. Ace's 8000 bodies outsize your 5000s; Kong Gun only reaches the 6-cost Marco and Vista (7 with OP17-097), and Marco can redirect removal onto itself. Race early and keep Loki as an 11000 blocker on their turn.

### vs OP14-041 Boa Hancock
Slightly Hancock. Her Trigger characters refill the board and draw on your turn. Avoid killing Kuja 5000+ bodies while her leader has DON!! if you are low on life.

### vs OP17-058 Kaido
Luffy favoured. Get under Kaido's ramp before turn 5; tempo matters more than Brook's discard here. Queen OP17-065's attack lock is the card that beats you.
