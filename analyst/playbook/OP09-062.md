## Game plan
Purple/yellow Big Mom Trigger midrange. The leader has [Banish], so its damage never lets the opponent's Triggers fire, and it ramps 1 rested DON!! per attack by pitching a Trigger card. The OP17 Big Mom package gives you 4000-power Trigger characters that play themselves from Life. Charlotte Linlin OP17-112 then turns them all into 8000s on your turn while also gaining you life or stripping theirs. You grind with life gain and Trigger value, then swing an oversized board.

Leader text: "[Banish] ... [When Attacking] You may trash 1 card with a [Trigger] from your hand: Add up to 1 DON!! card from your DON!! deck and rest it." Four life, 5000 power.

## Mulligan
- Keep: 1-cost Pudding or Olvia, a 3-cost (Pudding OP17-109, Daifuku, Yamato) and at least one Trigger card to pitch to the leader.
- Ramp pieces (Yamato, Miss Doublefinger) push Linlin a turn early; favour them going first.
- Ship: hands of only 6+ costs, and hands with no Trigger cards (the leader does nothing).

## Lines and cheese
- Every leader attack: pitch a spare Trigger card (extra Daifuku, a dead Olvia) for a rested DON!!. Rested DON!! can't pay costs this turn but is there, untapped, next turn: you are a turn ahead on DON!!.
- Turn 2-3: Yamato or Miss Doublefinger, leader attack with ramp. Aim for 8-9 DON!! by turn 4 going first.
- Linlin turn (10 DON!!): Linlin, choose life strip if racing or +1 Life if defending. Every 4000 Trigger body on board swings as 8000 this turn.
- Life math: Smoothie, Sweet 3 Generals, Borsalino, Teach and Linlin can add 3-5 Life over a game. Against aggro, choose life; against control, strip theirs.
- Trigger-proof damage: Banish leader hits, Robin OP09-107 and Linlin's strip all remove opposing Life without Triggers. Against Trigger decks (Hancock, Robin mirror), route damage through these.
- Lethal: Olvia gives the leader +3000; a Banish leader at 8000+ with Gum-Gum Giant held for defense is the usual closer.

## Matchups
Matchup calls are my judgement, informed by a ranked-play sample on opdecks.xyz (snapshot 2026-09-10) and card reading. Treat them as provisional.

### vs OP17-039 Rocks.D.Xebec
Rocks favoured. Captain John walls your leader, and Rocks out-draws your grind. Use Sweet 3 Generals' −3000 to break John, and Smoothie's discard to slow their hand.

### vs OP17-079 Monkey.D.Luffy
Slightly Luffy. Their 5000 swarm arrives before your Linlin turn; Yamato and Borsalino must hold early. Mont-d'or is live against their 1-cost Chopper/Nami/Rodo.

### vs OP13-004 Sabo
Even. Life gain is your edge; Sweet 3 Generals' double −3000 cleans up two 6000s. Don't let Saul live long.

### vs OP15-058 Enel
Slightly Robin. You out-scale Enel's 6 DON!! cap, and your 8000 bodies on your turn outsize its attackers. Its Lightning Dragon lock on Linlin hurts; bait it first.

### vs OP16-001 Portgas.D.Ace
Robin favoured. Life gain blunts Newgate double-attack turns, and Ace plays few Triggers, so your Banish hits are pure damage either way. Keep Borsalino for the double-attack turn.

### vs OP14-041 Boa Hancock
Robin clearly favoured. Banish switches off Hancock's whole Trigger engine on leader hits, and you take her Life without feeding her draws. Don't K.O. her Kuja 5000+ characters while her leader has DON!! attached.

### vs OP17-058 Kaido
Even. Kaido's Linlin ST34-004 (base power to 0) and Queen OP17-065 lock answer your big turn. Ramp harder and keep 2 threats.
