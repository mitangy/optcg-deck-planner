# OPTCG Deck Planner

Apps for One Piece Card Game players.

## Deck planner

[optcg-deck-planner.app](https://optcg-deck-planner.app)

Plan the decks you want to build and what it takes to finish them. Import decks, track the cards you own across all of them, and get a shopping list of what is still missing, priced with TCGPlayer market prices and recent sales. Pick alternate arts, watch price history and set price alerts, scan cards with your camera to update your collection, share lists by link, and run group buys with friends.

## Duel app

[optcgduel.app](https://optcgduel.app)

Play the One Piece Card Game online against friends, ranked opponents or bots. Build and edit decks (synced with the planner), spectate live matches, and watch or review replays of past games.

## Log Pose

An AI deck analyst built into both apps. Ask it about a deck, a matchup or a game you played, and it answers with card facts, draw odds, match statistics and tournament results.
